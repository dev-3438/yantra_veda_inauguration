About Yantra Veda

Yantra Veda is the robotics club of DPG ITM, created for students who want to move beyond theory and build intelligent machines that solve real-world problems.

Our mission is to bring together curious minds from across engineering and technology. Through teamwork, hands-on learning, and a culture of experimentation, Yantra Veda will provide a platform to explore robotics, artificial intelligence, automation, embedded systems, and future technolog ies.
